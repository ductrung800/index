      The page makes one cache-first JSONP request to action=intelligence. It does not scrape RS Radar, Group Momentum, Market Monitor or any other HTML page.

      Current-constituent equal-weight participation. Historical membership is not reconstructed, so survivorship bias is disclosed rather than hidden.

      Historical Replay

      Replay uses the verified 65-session S&P breadth cache plus available SPY/QQQ/VIX series. Current thematic leadership and Market Plan internals are not backfilled into prior dates.

Positions

New = stop below entry · Core = stop ≥ entry (financed) · rows recompute live
Methodology — adapted from Trader's Lab: "Portfolio-Level Risk Management".
  • Position Risk %EC = (Stop − Entry) × Shares ÷ Equity × 100. Negative while the stop sits below entry (unfinanced risk); zero or positive once the stop reaches or clears entry (financed — no downside remains against the original entry).
  • New (NE) vs Core (CE) is decided purely by that sign: Stop < Entry → New. Stop ≥ Entry → Core.
  • NER = Σ Position Risk %EC over New positions. NEP = Σ unrealized P/L %EC over New positions. NE Δ = NER + NEP is the control variable from §3: open a layer near your NER ceiling, keep pushing while NE Δ holds near half that ceiling, act (cut the weakest names) once it slides toward 1.5× the ceiling.
  • Open Heat = Σ Position Risk %EC over all open positions (worst case if every stop fires right now). Open Profit = Σ unrealized P/L %EC over all open positions. Portfolio Δ nets the two.
  • UER = %EC in open positions whose per-position return is below your unproven-return floor. FER = %EC in open positions where price sits within your fragile-distance threshold of the current stop. Both high at once is the configuration to fear (§6).
  • NE ÷ CE is the late-cycle risk budget (§4): keep it near your "typical" band when the tape is extended, allow it up toward the "stress-test ceiling" only when the market has actually earned it with a real reset, and treat the ceiling as a hard stop regardless of how clean new setups look.
All thresholds above are editable in the data panel — the framework is portable, the numbers are personal (§1). Nothing here is trading advice.
